    04 · Method

    Method

    1. One ITS sequence per species from NCBI GenBank.
    2. Pairwise global alignment. Distance = share of differing positions.
    3. Neighbour-joining tree, rooted on the Ascomycota.

    Limits

    • One sequence per species. A mislabelled record would move a branch.
    • ITS separates species well. Between orders it is near saturation, so deep branch order is unreliable.
    • No bootstrap support yet.

    Labels

    Edible, poisonous and inedible labels come from a hand-curated list with one source per species. None are generated by AI.
