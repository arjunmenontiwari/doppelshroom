Hi, I'm Arjun

I'm Arjun Menon Tiwari, a computer science student at the University of Helsinki with a minor in chemistry. Doppelshroom is my bioinformatics project, built around one question: are the poisonous mushrooms people mistake for edible ones actually related to them?

Why mushrooms

Mushroom lookalikes are a good puzzle for DNA. Two species can look almost the same and be distant relatives, or look different and be close cousins. Their barcodes show which.

They also matter. People get badly poisoned every year by mistaking one mushroom for another, which is why this site will never tell you a wild mushroom is safe to eat.

How it's built

Algorithms

Sequence alignment, neighbour-joining trees, and the k-mer index behind the DNA lens.

Statistics

Permutation tests on the analysis page, built so that pairs sharing a species don't count twice.

Data and AI

Records from GenBank, iNaturalist, GBIF and Wikipedia, with Gemini helping search and suggesting names from photos.

The site

Python and FastAPI, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, tested automatically on every change. No frameworks, no tracking.
