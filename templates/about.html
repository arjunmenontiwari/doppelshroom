Hi, I'm Arjun

I'm a computer science student, and Doppelshroom is my bioinformatics project. It started with one question: are the poisonous mushrooms people mistake for edible ones actually related to them?

Why mushrooms

Mushroom lookalikes are a good puzzle for DNA. Two species can look almost the same and be distant relatives, or look different and be close cousins. Their barcodes show which.

They also matter. People get badly poisoned every year by mistaking one mushroom for another, which is why this site will never tell you a wild mushroom is safe to eat.

How it's built

DNA

Barcodes from GenBank, lined up with Biopython, turned into distances and family trees.

Species

Names, photos and lookalikes from iNaturalist, labels from Wikipedia and a hand-checked list, maps from GBIF.

AI

Google Gemini turns descriptions and photos into species names, which are always checked against real records.

Site

Python and FastAPI on the server, plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in the browser. No frameworks, no tracking.

What's next

A lens with my own image model, trained on open mushroom photos, so the lens no longer depends on someone else's AI.
