Tree

The family tree

A tree drawn from DNA alone. It shows which of the ten species are close relatives and which are not.

How to read it
  1. Forks show relatives. Two species that join at a fork close to their names are close relatives. Species that only connect far to the left are distant.
  2. Length shows difference. The longer the lines between two species, the more their DNA differs.
  3. Brackets show lookalikes. Each numbered bracket joins an edible species to the one it gets confused with.

Select a species or a bracket to trace the path between a pair. Built from one DNA barcode per species. Method and limits

What it shows

Some lookalikes sit on the same small branch as their edible double. Others are on the far side of the tree and only resemble it.

Edible and poisonous species share branches. Being related to an edible mushroom says nothing about whether a species is safe.
