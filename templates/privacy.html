Privacy and terms

No accounts, no tracking, no cookies. This page lists everything that does leave your browser.

What this site keeps

Nothing about you. Searches by name, place or description are saved on the server only as their answers, so the same question is not asked of the databases twice. Your photos and your location are never saved.

The server that hosts the site may keep standard access logs, such as IP address and page address, for a short time to keep it running.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies. Your light or dark mode choice, whether Dopple is shown, and your best quiz streak are remembered in your browser's own storage and never sent anywhere. There is nothing to consent to, so there is no cookie banner.

Services this site uses

Google Gemini

Receives your photo on the Lens page and your text on "Describe it", to suggest species names. Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Gemini terms

iNaturalist

Photos load from its servers (hosted by Amazon Web Services), so they see your IP address. On "Near you", your location rounded to about 10 km is sent to it to find nearby records. Privacy policy

GBIF

Map tiles load from its servers, so it sees your IP address. Privacy policy

NCBI and Wikipedia

Asked by the server only. Your browser never contacts them unless you follow a link.

There are no analytics, ads or social media scripts. Fonts and the map library are served from this site, not from a third party.

Terms of use

Doppelshroom is an educational student project. It is not a mushroom identification tool and gives no advice you should act on. Edibility labels and AI-written facts can be wrong, and recipe links lead to outside sites this site does not check. Never eat a wild mushroom unless an expert has identified it in person.

The site is provided as it is, without any guarantee that it is correct, complete or available. Data, photos and text belong to their sources and are used under the licences listed on the sources page.

Questions or corrections: open an issue on GitHub.
