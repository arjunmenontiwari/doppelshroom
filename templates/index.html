The question

Are poisonous lookalikes close relatives, or just lookalikes?

The idea

Many edible mushrooms have a double: a different species that looks so similar that people mix them up. Some of those doubles are poisonous.

If two mushrooms look alike, are they related? Looks cannot answer that. DNA can.

This site compares one short stretch of DNA, called a barcode, from each species. The more two barcodes differ, the more distantly related the species are.
