The question

Are poisonous lookalikes close relatives, or just lookalikes?

Many edible mushrooms have a double that people mix them up with, and some doubles are poisonous. Looks cannot say whether two mushrooms are related. DNA can. Find any mushroom to see its lookalikes and how closely related they are.
