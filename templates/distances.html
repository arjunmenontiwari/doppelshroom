Distances

Every species against every other

Ten species make 45 possible pairs. Each square below is one comparison.

How to read it
  1. Find two species. Pick one from the rows and one from the numbered columns. The square where they meet compares them.
  2. Read the number. It is the percentage of DNA positions that differ between the two. 21 means 21 in every 100.
  3. Darker means closer. Dark squares are close relatives. Outlined squares are the five lookalike pairs.
