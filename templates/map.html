Where they are found

Two mushrooms can only be mixed up in places where both grow. These maps show where each one has been recorded.

How to read it
  1. Pick a pair. The edible species is on the left and its lookalike is on the right. The two maps move together.
  2. Lighter means more records. Each hexagon is coloured by how many times the species was recorded inside it.
  3. Empty does not mean absent. Records follow people. A place with few observers looks empty even where the species grows.
